UnderstoryDuo

Double bass and piano

Understory Duo is the double bassist Kellum Jones and the pianist Hsin Yeh.

We commission new work from living composers, and offer education for our community, showing the beauty of the double bass and the unique sound it makes together with the piano.

The understory is the new growth under the canopy of a forest. For us, it means making room for up-and-coming composers to grow the repertoire for double bass and piano. This music is our passion.
